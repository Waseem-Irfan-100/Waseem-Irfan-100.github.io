Things I've made

  1. 2026

    TRANSPO github ↗

    The campus bus app. React, FastAPI, SQLite behind Nginx. The institute now hosts it officially.

    what it taught me

    Once real people depend on something, you can't just push and see. A broken deploy in the morning means someone misses a bus.

    So releases build first and swap in one step, and if the health check fails they roll back on their own. When I had to restructure the live database (10 tables, 191 accounts) I ran it as a dry run inside a transaction first and kept the old login tokens working. Nobody got logged out.

    The schedule itself was harder than it looks. Trips that start before midnight and end after it broke my first search algorithm.

  2. 2026

    Forest fire intelligence engine github ↗

    Open-source fire-weather engine and map for India's forests, from my research internship.

    the 87-point bug

    One number, the hourly Drought Code, kept drifting away from the official reference. Mean error: 87.46. Everything else matched.

    Finding why took longer than writing the model. I stopped guessing and checked each step against the reference until I found where they split. After the fix the error was 0.23.

    Trust the tests over your gut. I still think about that one.

  3. 2026

    Prompt-injection red team github ↗

    I threw 35 attack templates at an LLM app and 38% got through. After four rounds of fixes, a hardened system prompt and an output classifier, 14% did.
